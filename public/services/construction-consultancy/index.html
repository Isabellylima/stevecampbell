Construction Claims & Consultancy

Claims, delay analysis, contract advice and project management from a Chartered Construction Manager with more than 40 years in the industry.

Claims preparation and defence

Well-prepared claims settle; poorly prepared ones become disputes. Steve prepares and responds to claims for extensions of time, loss and expense, variations and final accounts, setting out cause and effect clearly and supporting it with contemporaneous records.

Delay analysis and extensions of time

Steve carries out forensic planning and retrospective delay analysis using recognised methods, selected to suit the records available and the question to be answered:

  • Time impact analysis
  • Windows analysis
  • Collapsed as-built ("as-built but for")
  • Impacted as-planned
  • As-planned versus as-built

He also advises on whether time has been set "at large" and on the interaction between extensions of time and liquidated damages.

Contract advice

  • Choosing and amending standard forms — JCT, NEC, FIDIC
  • Interpretation of clauses and reconciling ambiguities and inconsistencies
  • Payment, notice and dispute resolution provisions
  • Tendering and procurement processes
  • Valuation mechanisms, including quantum meruit as an alternative

Project management, contract administration and cost

Drawing on experience of projects from around £10,000 to more than £80 million, Steve provides project and construction management, contract administration and quantity surveying services — including cost planning, valuations, variation pricing and final accounts. He is known for taking on difficult projects and bringing troubled ones back under control.

Dispute avoidance

A pre-contract review of payment terms, notice provisions and dispute clauses, or an early independent look at a developing problem, is often the most cost-effective advice a client receives.

Frequently asked questions

Which delay analysis method is best?

There is no single best method. The right choice depends on the contract, the quality of the programme and records, the stage of the project and the question being asked. A method that suits a prospective extension of time claim may not suit a retrospective dispute.

Can you help before a dispute starts?

Yes. Pre-contract advice and early review of claims, notices and programmes are often the most effective way to avoid a formal dispute.

Which contract forms do you work with?

JCT, NEC and FIDIC standard forms, as well as bespoke contracts.

Discuss your matter in confidence

Tell Steve what has happened and where things stand. He will explain the realistic options — including whether a formal process is needed at all. The initial consultation is free.
