International

Construction disputes and consultancy for projects in Portugal

Independent arbitration, mediation and construction expertise for owners, developers, investors and contractors with projects in Portugal — and for Portugal-based clients with matters in the UK.

Who this is for

  • UK and international owners and investors building or renovating in Portugal
  • Developers and contractors working under international or English-language contracts
  • Portuguese companies contracting with UK or international counterparties
  • Clients living in Portugal with construction or property matters in England and Wales

Services available

International arbitration

Steve accepts appointments as arbitrator in construction and property disputes with an international element, and represents parties in arbitration. Awards are enforceable in both Portugal and the UK under the New York Convention. An arbitration seated in Portugal is governed by the Portuguese Voluntary Arbitration Law (Law no. 63/2011).

Mediation

Confidential mediation for cross-border construction and property disputes, where a negotiated outcome is quicker and more practical than formal proceedings in either country.

Independent construction expertise

Technical reports on construction quality, defects, delay and cost, based on more than 40 years of building and project management experience — for use in negotiations, mediations, arbitrations, or to support a client's Portuguese legal advisers.

Contract and project advice

Advice on FIDIC and other international standard forms, claims for time and money, valuations and final accounts, and project management or review for clients who want an independent view of how their project is being run.

Scope. Steve Campbell is not a Portuguese lawyer and does not advise on Portuguese law. Where a matter requires Portuguese legal advice, clients should instruct an advogado; Steve regularly works alongside clients' legal advisers as the construction specialist.

Frequently asked questions

Can a UK arbitrator act in a dispute about a project in Portugal?

Yes, where the parties agree. Parties to an international contract can choose their arbitrators, and arbitrators need not be nationals of, or qualified in, the country where the project is located. The procedural law depends on the seat of the arbitration.

Does UK construction law apply to my project in Portugal?

Generally not. The Party Wall etc. Act 1996 and the statutory adjudication scheme apply to work and contracts within their scope in the UK. Contracts for projects in Portugal are usually governed by Portuguese law unless the parties have validly chosen otherwise. Questions of Portuguese law should be put to a lawyer qualified in Portugal.

Can Steve help with a UK dispute while I live in Portugal?

Yes. Many owners and businesses based in Portugal have property, projects or contracts in the UK. Steve can act in UK adjudication, arbitration, mediation and party wall matters regardless of where the client lives.
