What the e-book covers

  • The Party Wall etc. Act 1996 — the three types of work it covers, notices, the 14-day rule, agreed and third surveyors, awards and schedules of condition
  • Construction adjudication — the statutory right to adjudicate under the Construction Act, the 2011 amendments, and why householder contracts are treated differently
  • Mediation — how a facilitated negotiation works and why it suits construction disputes
  • Expert determination — when a binding expert decision is the right tool for a technical question
  • Arbitration and early neutral evaluation — how they differ from adjudication and from each other
  • Expert witnesses — their duties and the kinds of construction claims they address
  • Extensions of time and delay analysis — the main analytical methods and claims preparation

The e-book reflects the law and practice at the time it was written. Legislation and case law change, so take advice on the current position before acting on any specific matter.
