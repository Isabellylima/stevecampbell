About Steve Campbell

Chartered Construction Manager, international arbitrator, construction adjudicator, mediator, expert witness and party wall surveyor. DipAdj FCIArb FFPWS MCIOB.

From the trades to the tribunal

Steve Campbell has more than 40 years of experience across all sectors of the construction industry. He started as a tradesman, physically building houses, extensions and commercial units, and working on refurbishment, alterations and maintenance. He then progressed through site management and project management to construction director.

Along the way he has managed projects valued from around £10,000 to more than £80 million, from inception to completion, under JCT, NEC, FIDIC and bespoke contracts and a range of procurement routes. He has carried out project management, contract administration and quantity surveying, and is known for taking on difficult and complex projects and rescuing those in trouble.

Dispute resolution since 2009

Since 2009 Steve has concentrated on construction and property dispute resolution. He acts as adjudicator, arbitrator, mediator, party representative and expert witness, and has appeared in the county court, the Technology and Construction Court, and in arbitrations, adjudications and mediations.

He arbitrates and adjudicates disputes between clients and contractors, clients and professional consultants, and contractors and subcontractors, and is regularly selected as a third surveyor in party wall matters.

Teaching and writing

Steve lectures and writes on party wall and boundary matters, arbitration, adjudication, jurisdiction, easements and occupiers' liability, and has presented seminars to construction and property professionals.

Approach

The crest of the practice carries the motto sine timore vel favore — without fear or favour. Whether appointed independently or instructed by one party, Steve's aim is the same: to understand the building, the people who built it and the commercial pressures on them, and to reach a clear, practical answer.
